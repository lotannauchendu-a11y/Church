What we believe

Anchored in the gospel

The good news of Jesus Christ is at the center of everything we do.

The Bible

The Bible is the inspired, true, and sufficient Word of God. It is our final authority for what we believe and how we live.

2 Timothy 3:16–17 · 2 Peter 1:21

God

There is one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. He is holy, loving, and sovereign over all things.

Matthew 28:19 · Psalm 115:3

Jesus Christ

Jesus is fully God and fully man. He lived a sinless life, died on the cross for sinners, rose from the dead, and reigns as Lord.

John 1:1, 14 · 1 Corinthians 15:3–4

The Holy Spirit

The Holy Spirit gives new life, helps us understand God’s Word, and makes us more like Jesus.

John 3:5–8 · Galatians 5:22–25

Our need

God made us in his image, but we have all sinned and turned from him. On our own, we cannot save ourselves.

Genesis 1:27 · Romans 3:23

Salvation

Salvation is a gift of God’s grace, received through faith in Jesus alone. Everyone is called to turn from sin and trust him.

Ephesians 2:8–9 · Acts 3:19

The church

The church is God’s family of believers. We gather to worship, celebrate baptism and the Lord’s Supper, and make disciples of all nations.

Acts 2:42 · Matthew 28:18–20

Christ’s return

Jesus will return to judge the living and the dead and to make all things new.

Acts 17:31 · Revelation 21:5
