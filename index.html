Who we are

We exist to treasure Jesus above all and make disciples who make disciples.

Calvary is the hill where Jesus died for sinners. Cedar Hill is the city he has sent us to love. Our name holds the two together.

  • The Bible is God’s Word: true, sufficient, and our final authority.
  • The gospel saves by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone.
  • The church is a family that worships, grows, and goes together.
