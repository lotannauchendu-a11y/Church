Our new name

Why Calvary Hill?

Calvary is the hill outside Jerusalem where Jesus was crucified. There the Son of God gave his life for sinners, and three days later he rose from the dead. Everything we are as a church flows from that hill.

Cedar Hill is the city God has given us to love. Our new name holds the two together: the good news of Calvary, planted in the middle of our community.

“But far be it from me to boast except in the cross of our Lord Jesus Christ.”

Galatians 6:14

Our mission

Treasure Jesus above all

Jesus is worth more than anything this world can offer. We want our worship, our homes, our time, and our plans to show that he is our greatest joy.

Plan your visit

Your first Sunday

Visiting a new church can feel like a big step. Here’s what to expect, so you can walk in knowing what’s ahead.

When
Sundays at 10:00 AM. Come a few minutes early to get settled and check in your kids.
Worship
We sing, pray, read Scripture together, and hear a sermon that works through a book of the Bible, passage by passage.
Kids
Calvary Hill Kids begins with a nursery for babies and toddlers, with classes where children hear the good news of Jesus every week. Our volunteers are trained to keep your kids safe.
What to wear
Whatever you’re comfortable in. You’ll see jeans, and you’ll see Sunday clothes.
Afterward
Stay and say hello. Our pastors would love to meet you, pray with you, and answer any questions.

What we believe

Anchored in the gospel

These are the truths we build our lives on. We are part of the Acts 29 Network, a global family of church-planting churches.

The Bible

Scripture is the inspired, inerrant, and sufficient Word of God, our final authority for what we believe and how we live.

2 Timothy 3:16–17 · 2 Peter 1:21

God

There is one God, eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit. He is sovereign over all things, including our salvation.

Matthew 28:19 · Ephesians 1:4–5

The Gospel

Salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone, to the glory of God alone. Jesus calls everyone to repent and believe.

Ephesians 2:8–9 · Romans 1:16–17

New Life

The Holy Spirit gives new birth, unites us to Christ, and keeps every one of his people to the end.

John 3:3–8 · Philippians 1:6

The Church

The church is a gathered body of baptized believers, shepherded by elders and sent to make disciples of all nations.

Matthew 28:18–20 · 1 Peter 5:1–4

Christ’s Return

Jesus will return in glory to judge the living and the dead and to make all things new.

Acts 17:31 · Revelation 21:5
