Sermon Rehearsal

Paste your sermon from Logos and hear it read back, sentence by sentence, so it moves from the page into your heart.

Bring in a sermon

  1. In Logos, open your sermon and click into the text.
  2. Press ⌘A to select it all, then ⌘C to copy.
  3. Click the box below and press ⌘V. You can also drop a .txt, .docx, .rtf or .md file here.
