Calvary Hill Kids

Helping kids know Jesus.

Every Sunday, kids learn the good news of Jesus in a safe, caring place.

Photo · Kids on Sunday

On Sundays

What your kids can expect

During the 10:00 AM service, babies and toddlers (birth to 23 months) are cared for in our nursery, and kids meet in classes where they hear Bible stories, sing, and learn about Jesus.

Safe

Our volunteers are trained to care for your kids, and you’ll check them in when you arrive.

Gospel-centered

Every lesson points kids to Jesus and what he has done for them.

With parents

We want to help you teach your kids about Jesus at home, too.

At home

Pray together with ACTS.

A simple way to pray as a family at dinner or bedtime. Give each child a turn. They don’t need big words to talk to their heavenly Father.
