About

Who we are

A church in Cedar Hill that exists to treasure Jesus above all and make disciples who make disciples.

Our mission

Two things we live for

Treasure Jesus above all

Jesus is worth more than anything this world can offer. We want our worship, our homes, and our plans to show that he is our greatest joy.

“I count everything as loss because of the surpassing worth of knowing Christ Jesus my Lord.”

Philippians 3:8

Make disciples who make disciples

Following Jesus was never meant to stop with us. We help one another grow in God’s Word and prayer, then pass it on to our kids, our friends, and our neighbors.

“What you have heard from me … entrust to faithful men, who will be able to teach others also.”

2 Timothy 2:2

Our name

Why Calvary Hill?

Calvary is the hill where Jesus died for sinners and rose again three days later. Cedar Hill is the city he has sent us to love. Our name, and the two hills in our logo, hold them together.
